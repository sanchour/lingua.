## Storage and repairs

No new dependency, database table or migration. Text rows keep marks, confirmed glossary and generated
attribution; journal rows keep image references and snapshots. Existing backup/restore policies carry
these fields; binary images require media-inclusive backups and do not travel in ordinary text-only sync.

- Chapter rebuilding now tokenises before the atomic Undo transaction. Browsers previously failed with
  `TransactionInactiveError` when the vocabulary rebuild yielded inside it. Counts/vocabulary/stats and
  chapter edits now commit together; a changed book is rejected rather than overwritten.
- Note flushing uses the latest saved mark metadata: leaving immediately after favouriting and typing
  a note must not silently remove the favourite flag.
- Narration keeps its controls mounted while playing and cancels on Stop, navigation, hiding the page
  or another reader interaction. It follows existing word spans without changing word states.
- Exact source anchors remain checked for highlights/parallel text. A chapter title/index is not a
  semantic alignment algorithm; an edited source is not silently translated or reinterpreted.
